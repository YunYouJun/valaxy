Valaxy 1.3.5 updates the Yun theme's homepage navigation and common controls.

- Set `banner.navStyle: 'glass'` for frosted navigation links on desktop and mobile. Use `pages[].iconColor` to color icons independently.
- Homepage social icons show their brand colors on hover and keyboard focus. Article cards, collection cards, tags, and pagination use updated borders and hover colors.
- Returning to the top interrupts an in-progress scroll. The scroll controls respect reduced motion, and glass surfaces use a solid background with increased contrast or reduced transparency.
